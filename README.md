# LearnLoop Marketing Analytics Platform

**End-to-End Marketing Analytics Project | SQL · Tableau · Snowflake · dbt · Python · Airflow · AWS S3**

---

## About LearnLoop

**LearnLoop** is a simulated subscription-based EdTech SaaS company that provides online courses and corporate training programs.

The company acquires customers through digital marketing campaigns, converts website visitors into leads, sells monthly subscription plans, and provides online courses to its subscribers.

As LearnLoop grows, customer data is generated across several independent platforms:

- **Google Ads & Meta Ads** — advertising spend, impressions, clicks, and campaign performance
- **Google Analytics 4** — website sessions, traffic sources, devices, and conversions
- **HubSpot** — leads, MQLs, SQLs, customers, and acquisition sources
- **Stripe** — subscriptions, payments, plans, and recurring revenue
- **LearnLoop Course Platform** — course enrollments and completions

This creates an important analytics problem.

---

## The Business Problem

LearnLoop's marketing, website, CRM, subscription, payment, and course data are stored in **separate systems**.

Because these systems are disconnected, the company cannot easily follow the complete customer journey from the first advertising interaction to the revenue ultimately generated by that customer.

For example, the marketing team can see that a campaign generated thousands of clicks, but that alone does not answer:

- Did those clicks generate leads?
- Did those leads become qualified leads?
- How many eventually became customers?
- How much did it cost to acquire those customers?
- How much revenue did those customers generate?
- Which campaigns acquired the highest-value customers?
- Where are potential customers dropping out of the acquisition funnel?

The core business problem is therefore:

> **How can LearnLoop connect marketing spend with customer acquisition, subscriptions, and revenue so decision-makers can understand which marketing activities actually create business value?**

To answer this question, LearnLoop needs to connect the complete customer journey:

**Marketing Spend → Website Sessions → Leads → MQLs → SQLs → Customers → Subscriptions → Revenue → Course Engagement**

---

## The Solution

I built an **end-to-end Marketing Analytics Platform** that integrates LearnLoop's marketing, website, CRM, subscription, payment, and course data into a centralized analytics environment.

The solution:

1. Extracts source data using **Python**
2. Stores raw JSON data in **AWS S3**
3. Centralizes the data in **Snowflake**
4. Cleans and transforms the data using **dbt**
5. Builds dimensional fact and dimension tables
6. Calculates business KPIs using **SQL**
7. Validates data through automated **dbt tests**
8. Orchestrates the pipeline with **Apache Airflow**
9. Presents the final analysis through interactive **Tableau dashboards**

The result is a centralized analytical view that allows LearnLoop to evaluate marketing performance based on **customers, recurring revenue, and customer value**, rather than advertising activity alone.

## Business Questions

The platform was designed to answer practical questions that marketing and business teams could use for decision-making.

### Marketing Performance

- Which campaigns generate the most revenue?
- Which campaigns have the highest **ROAS**?
- What is the **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**?
- Which campaigns acquire customers most efficiently?
- How are advertising spend and revenue changing over time?

### Customer Acquisition & Funnel

- How many website sessions become leads?
- How many leads become **Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)**?
- How many MQLs become **Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs)**?
- How many qualified leads ultimately become customers?
- Where are the largest funnel drop-offs?
- How is funnel performance changing over time?

### Subscription & Customer Value

- How many subscriptions are being generated?
- How many subscriptions are active?
- What are the current **MRR** and **ARR**?
- How is recurring revenue changing over time?
- Which subscription plans generate the most MRR?
- What is the average **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**?
- Which campaigns acquire the highest-value customers?

### Course Performance

- Which courses receive the most enrollments?
- Which course categories attract the most students?
- What percentage of enrollments are completed?
- Which courses have the highest completion rates?
- How are enrollments changing over time?

# Tableau Dashboards

The final analytical solution contains **four Tableau dashboards**, each designed around a specific business decision area.

---

## 1. Campaign Performance

**Business Goal:** Determine which marketing campaigns produce the strongest financial results and where marketing budget should be allocated.

### Key KPIs

- Total Ad Spend
- Total Revenue
- ROAS
- CAC

### Analysis

- Revenue by Campaign
- ROAS by Campaign
- Spend vs. Revenue
- Performance Over Time

This dashboard connects advertising investment with downstream customer and revenue outcomes.

Instead of evaluating campaigns only through impressions and clicks, LearnLoop can evaluate whether marketing investment is actually producing revenue and customers.

---

## 2. Marketing Funnel

**Business Goal:** Understand how effectively prospects move through the acquisition funnel and identify where potential customers are being lost.

The dashboard tracks:

**Sessions → Leads → MQLs → SQLs → Customers**

### Key KPIs

- Total Sessions
- Total Leads
- Total MQLs
- Total SQLs
- Total Customers

### Analysis

- Funnel progression
- Stage-to-stage conversion
- Funnel drop-off
- Conversion trends over time

This allows LearnLoop to identify which stages of the acquisition process represent the largest opportunities for conversion improvement.

---

## 3. Subscription & Customer Value

**Business Goal:** Understand recurring-revenue performance and determine the long-term value of acquired customers.

### Key KPIs

- Total Subscriptions
- Active Subscriptions
- MRR
- ARR
- Average Customer Lifetime Value

### Analysis

- MRR Over Time
- MRR by Subscription Plan
- Subscriptions by Plan
- Customer Value by Campaign

This dashboard extends the analysis beyond customer acquisition.

A campaign that generates many customers is not necessarily the most valuable campaign if those customers generate little revenue.

By connecting marketing acquisition with subscription and customer value, LearnLoop can evaluate the **quality of acquired customers**, not only the quantity.

---

## 4. Course Performance

**Business Goal:** Understand how customers engage with LearnLoop's educational products after acquisition.

### Key KPIs

- Total Enrollments
- Total Completions

- Completion Rate

### Analysis

- Enrollments by Course
- Enrollments by Category
- Completion Rate by Course
- Enrollments Over Time

This adds a product-engagement perspective to the analysis by showing whether acquired customers actually use and complete LearnLoop's courses.

# ETL & Analytics Pipeline

## 1. Data Ingestion — Python

Python scripts handle data ingestion and preparation for the different source systems.

The ingestion layer includes:

- Data extraction
- Validation
- Logging
- S3 uploads
- Pipeline execution

---

## 2. Raw Storage — AWS S3

Extracted data is stored as raw JSON files in **AWS S3**.

This creates a raw storage layer that separates source ingestion from downstream transformation.

---

## 3. Cloud Data Warehouse — Snowflake

Raw data is loaded into **Snowflake**, which acts as the centralized cloud data warehouse.

Marketing, website, CRM, subscription, payment, and course data can therefore be analyzed within a common environment.

---

## 4. Data Transformation — dbt

dbt transforms the raw Snowflake data through several analytical layers:

```text
Raw Data
    │
    ▼
Staging
    │
    ▼
Intermediate
    │
    ▼
Marts
    │
    ▼
Analytics / KPI Models
```

### Staging Layer

Source data is cleaned, standardized, renamed, and validated.

Models include:

- `stg_google_ads`
- `stg_meta_ads`
- `stg_ga4_sessions`
- `stg_hubspot_contacts`
- `stg_stripe_payments`
- `stg_stripe_subscriptions`
- `stg_course_enrollments`

### Intermediate Layer

Intermediate models combine multiple sources and implement reusable business logic.

Models include:

- `int_marketing_performance`
- `int_marketing_attribution`
- `int_customer_acquisition`
- `int_customer_revenue`
- `int_subscription_metrics`

### Marts Layer

The marts layer organizes analytical data into dimensional fact and dimension tables optimized for analysis.

### Analytics / KPI Layer

Business-ready analytical models prepare the metrics consumed by Tableau:

- `kpi_campaign_performance`
- `kpi_funnel_performance`
- `kpi_subscription_performance`
- `kpi_customer_value`
- `kpi_course_performance`

This architecture keeps important business logic in the data layer instead of duplicating complex calculations across Tableau worksheets.
